Terrain palettes are divided into rows, from top to bottom:
    1 - Sunlit background
    2 - Sunlit midground
    3 - Sunlit foreground
    4 - Shaded background
    5 - Shaded midground
    6 - Shaded foreground
    7 - Colors

The first 6 rows are the terrain's main color gradients, and go from dark on the left to bright on the right.
If you want more control based on depth, you can add more rows. Just make sure there are the same number of lit and unlit rows!
Similarly, palettes can be made wider to give more control over lighting.


The "Colors" row is organized from left to right:
    1 - Glitter color     (visible at certain angles in bright sunlight)
    2 - Light tint        (multiplier/tint for lights cast on terrain, currently unused)
    3 - Dark dust color   (sand puffs in shadow)
    4 - Light dust color  (sand puffs in sunlight)
    5 - Sandstorm color   (sandstorm end-of-cycle effect, or black to use sky color)
